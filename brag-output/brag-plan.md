# Brag Plan: FocusLinks — Feature Showreel (6 spots)

## What is this app?
FocusLinks is the professional network for optometrists — a verified peer directory,
interest circles with a central event board, specialty consult hubs, 24-hour clinical
stories, a mixed explore wall and a connections-first feed. It is optometry's home on
the internet, built by and for ODs.

## The angle
Every feature gets its own **advertisement spot** with a real storyline — hook, proof,
punchline — following the brag creative laws. The landing section is a **screening
room**: one continuous showreel that plays spot after spot like a brand film, not a
grid of feature cards. The ads never describe the product; they *show the thing* —
the real interface with real data, simulated taps, typed queries, live counters.

## The brag contract (creative laws applied)
- **The hook is everything.** Every spot slams a big typographic hook in the first
  2 seconds — the pain point, stated like a trailer line.
- **Show the thing.** After the hook wipes away, the real UI carries the spot:
  typed searches, cursor taps, numbers ticking, cards flipping.
- **Readable.** Fast-in then HOLD. Hook words slam in ~0.17s apart and hold the full
  hook window. Captions are the voiceover script.
- **Every frame postable.** The hook frame, the proof frames and the punchline frame
  each stand alone as a still.
- **Specific.** Real doctors (Amara Thorne 99%, Maya Lin 93%), real events (Scleral
  Symposium · Oct 14 · 2.0 COPE · 342 RSVP), real consults (CSR vs occult Type 1 CNV),
  real stories (Slit Lamp: Salzmann Nodule), real posts (myopia poll 325 votes).
- **No generic SaaS language.** Banned: "streamline", "supercharge", "elevate".

## Shared spot structure (all six)
```
HOOK  0.0–2.4s   full-bleed cinematic takeover inside the app frame:
                 eyebrow + trailer line, words slam in one-by-one
WIPE  2.4–3.05s  hook lifts up (dramatic wipe) revealing the real UI
PROOF 3.05–D-2.1s  the UI doing its job (simulated interaction, counters,
                   one KINETIC BEAT type-slam near the end)
PUNCH D-2.1–D    UI dims under a brand lockup: FocusLinks · <feature> · tagline
```
Landing cut: 13.5–15s per spot (six spots ≈ 84s showreel, auto-toured). Tighter than
the standalone 15–25s law because the reel is skippable and loops; the hook/reveal/
punchline contract is kept intact.

## Visual identity (from the product)
- Background: `#0A0A0F` cinema frame (both themes); app surfaces `#101014` / white
- Accent per spot: Directory blue-600 · Circles emerald-500 · Consults violet-600 ·
  Spotlight fuchsia-500 · Explore amber-500 · Feed sky-500
- Text: neutral-900 / white; captions neutral-300
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans (black, tracking-tight) — same as landing
- Brand mark: Eye glyph in blue→indigo→sky gradient tile + emerald status dot
- Strongest visual element: the real app chrome — AppHeaderBar, traffic-light window,
  verified shields, emerald membership chip `FL-29841-OD`

## Voice / audio direction (silent cut)
No audio files on the landing page; the **caption strip is the voiceover script** and
the kinetic type carries the rhythm. Motion pacing is written beat-tight so a future
music bed can drop straight onto the storyboards below.
